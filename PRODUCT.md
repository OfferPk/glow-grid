## One-liner

Place glowing block shapes on a grid, clear rows and columns, ride combo streaks — a tiny offline neon puzzle PWA.

## MVP scope (§5)

1. 8×8 playfield  
2. Tray of up to 3 polyominoes (~16 defs)  
3. Drag + tap-cell + rotate (button / R)  
4. Full row **and** column clear (static)  
5. Locked combo scoring  
6. Game over when no tray piece fits  
7. localStorage persistence  
8. Daily seeded challenge (Asia/Karachi)  
9. Neon polish + mute + vibrate  
10. PWA shell (manifest, icons, SW)

## Non-goals

Multiplayer, accounts, ads, power-ups, native stores.
